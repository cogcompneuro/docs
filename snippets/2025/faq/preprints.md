### Can I preprint the manuscript that I plan to submit to CCN?

Papers to both tracks must be submitted fully anonymized.
Authors may disseminate their work publicly under their names (e.g., on a
preprint server), but are asked to refrain from publicizing (*e.g.*, on social
media) work under review at CCN until CCN decisions are released for all
tracks ({{ proceedings_decisions_2025 }}) to prevent accidental de-anonymization during the
review process.
