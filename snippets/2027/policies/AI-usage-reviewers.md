Artificial intelligence (AI) may only be used by reviewers for **assistance**
purposes, such as organizing your written text, polishing the language of your
review, and clarifying concepts that you want to understand better. The
scientific content of your review must stem from your own discernment; do not
outsource your judgment to an LLM.

Separately, submission content (including text and figures, substantive
paraphrases thereof, as well as ideas, results, code) must not be entered into
any system that does not guarantee zero data retention as part of its terms of
service, as this constitutes a breach of confidentiality (see above). The
default consumer interfaces of ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini do not guarantee zero
data retention; when in doubt, do not upload or paste.

Note that polishing your review's language can still expose submission content
if you paraphrase the manuscript. Either confirm your tool meets the
confidentiality bar above, or limit AI assistance to portions of your review
that do not concern the submission's content.
