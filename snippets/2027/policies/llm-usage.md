reviewers take full responsibility for the content written under their name,
including any content generated by LLMs and any content that could be
construed as plagiarism or scientific misconduct (e.g., fabrication of facts).

tools, including large language models, in their manuscript. Papers that make
extensive use of LLMs and do not disclose this usage will be desk-rejected.
